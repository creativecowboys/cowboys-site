# AI SEO — what the $97/mo add-on covers

Set October 15, 2026 (Josh). $97/mo added to Local Growth or Max Growth, or on its own for any website we built and
host (Website Hosting & Support). The $297 standalone price for sites we don't host is retired: we only sell this
where we own the code. Priced in `src/lib/packages/catalog.ts` (`ai-seo`).

**The goal:** when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Google's AI "who's a good roofer in Carrollton?", the
client is named and their site is the source quoted. Those answers come from search results (ChatGPT leans on Bing,
AI Overviews on Google), listings and review sites, and pages that state facts plainly.

## Month 1 (setup, included)
- **Structured data on every page**: business type, services, service area, hours, FAQs, rating.
- **AI crawlers allowed**: the ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity bots are not blocked.
- **Bing Places listing** claimed or matched (it imports from Google; the client may need to sign in once).
- **Facts match everywhere**: name, address, phone, hours and services on the site, Google, Bing, Facebook and Yelp.
- **Baseline**: 15 questions people actually ask about the trade and town, checked in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI
  Overviews and Gemini (SearchAtlas LLM Visibility).

## Every month
1. Re-run the 15 questions: named, cited or missing, and which competitors were named.
2. Add **one answer section** to the site for a question they're losing: an FAQ block or service-page section that
   answers in its first sentence. Not a blog post (blog content stays in the SEO packages).
3. Fix one or two of the sources AI cites for their trade and town (Yelp, Angi, local "best of" lists, Chamber).
4. Keep facts current: hours, services and prices on the site and in its structured data.
5. A one-page report: "Named in 6 of 15 AI answers (up from 2). Competitor X: 9."

## Not included (Local Growth)
Google Business Profile management, review generation, listings at scale, blog content. When the report shows the
client losing on reviews or Google, that is the case for Local Growth.

## How we keep it at $97
About 45 minutes of staff time per client per month. SearchAtlas runs the tracking; Claude drafts the answer section
and the report from that data; we approve both.

**What we tell clients:** AI answers change from one search to the next, so we never guarantee mentions. We promise
the work, the tracking and the trend.
